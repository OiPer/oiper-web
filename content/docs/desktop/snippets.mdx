title: Snippets
description: Expand short spoken shortcuts into longer text.
---

A snippet replaces a short shortcut with longer text. Saying "brb" inserts
"be right back"; saying your company shortcut inserts its full legal name.

Snippets are applied last, after transcription and any [formatting](/docs/desktop/formatting),
just before the text is inserted.

## Creating a snippet

On the **Snippets** page, choose **Add new** and fill in two fields:

- **Shortcut** — the text to look for, such as `brb`.
- **Expands to** — the text that replaces it, such as `be right back`.

Snippets apply to every profile. Use the row actions to edit or delete one later.

## How matching works

- Shortcuts are **not case-sensitive**: `brb`, `Brb`, and `BRB` all match.
- Two snippets cannot share the same shortcut, ignoring case and surrounding spaces. The
  dialog blocks a duplicate before you can save it.
- Replacement text is inserted exactly as you wrote it, and is never itself scanned for
  snippets. A snippet cannot trigger another one.

Matching is not limited to whole words, so a shortcut also matches inside a longer word.
Keep shortcuts distinctive — `br` would also fire inside "brown". For the same reason, avoid
shortcuts where one is the beginning of another, such as `cat` and `catalog`, since which
one wins is not guaranteed.
