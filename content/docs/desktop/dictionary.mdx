---
title: Dictionary
description: Teach OiPer names, jargon, and terms it keeps getting wrong.
---

The dictionary is a list of words you often say that speech models tend to mishear —
personal and product names, company names, technical terms, and acronyms. The list is passed
to the speech model as a hint, which makes it far more likely to spell them the way you
intended.

## Adding words

On the **Dictionary** page, type a word and add it. Add the exact spelling you want in the
output, including capitalisation, for example `PostgreSQL` rather than `postgresql`.

Short multi-word phrases work too if that is how you say them.

## Size limit

The whole dictionary is limited to **896 characters**, counted as each word's length plus
three characters of overhead. The page shows how much of the budget you have used, and
stops you once it is full.

Because the budget is small, spend it on words the model actually gets wrong. Ordinary
vocabulary does not need to be listed.

## Which modes use it

The dictionary is applied in **cloud** and **oiper** modes.

Local Whisper transcription does not currently use it. If you dictate in `local` mode and a
name keeps coming out wrong, use a [snippet](/docs/desktop/snippets) to rewrite the common
misspelling instead, or move that profile to a larger Whisper model.
